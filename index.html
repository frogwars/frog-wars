Frog Wars

Health: 100 Score: 0 Time: 0s Level: 1 Weapon: Poison Darts

Island Survival

You are an orange poison dart frog. Survive the squid swarm on your island with the help of your computer toad ally.

  • Move with WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Collect glowing berries to recover health
  • Keep the squids away from your island
  • Switch weapons with Q/E or the mouse wheel
